FAQ

Good questions, honest answers.

Do you take my insurance?

We're in-network with most major PPO plans. Give us a call or include your plan when booking and we'll verify your benefits before your visit, free of charge.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. We offer noise-canceling headphones, weighted blankets and nitrous oxide ("laughing gas"). Tell us when you book and we'll plan extra time for you.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call us right away. We reserve time every day for emergencies, and most patients are seen the same day.

How much does a visit cost without insurance?

New patients can take advantage of our $99 special. For ongoing care, our Smile Membership covers preventive visits for $29/month.
